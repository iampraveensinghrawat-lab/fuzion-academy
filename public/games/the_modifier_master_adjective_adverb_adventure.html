Grammar Guide: Modifiers

1. What is an Adjective?

An adjective modifies or describes a noun or pronoun (e.g., The fierce lion roared). It answers: What kind? Which one? How many?

2. What is an Adverb?

An adverb modifies a verb, an adjective, or another adverb (e.g., She ran quickly or It was extremely cold). Often ends in -ly and answers: How? When? Where? To what extent?

3. Lengthening Sentences

By adding descriptive adjectives and precise adverbs, you transform short, boring sentences into vivid, lengthy, professional English sentences!
